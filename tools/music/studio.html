Tomorrowind studio

The song

Each block plays a section. Drag blocks to change the order, or drag a section card in from below. Click a block to set who plays in it.

Sections

The pieces the song is made of. Open one in Compose to write its chords, melody and drums.

Drag a track to the part it plays in this section, or click it and then click the row. Gold: not its usual part.

Note length
The whole melody

Click an empty spot for a note; double-click a note to take it away. Click notes to select them (Shift for more), or drag a box around them. Drag them to move, with Option to copy; drag a note's right edge to make it longer. Keys: Delete, arrows (Shift+↑↓: an octave), ⌘C ⌘V, ⌘D duplicate, ⌘A all. Gold rows are the notes of the chord playing there.

Click a square for a hit on that half beat. Timpani play the root of the chord.

The band

Each track plays one part with one sound, in every section. In Arrange you choose who plays in each block.
