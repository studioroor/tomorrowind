Tomorrowind studio

    ↑↓ to choose · Enter to do it · Esc to close · ⌘K to open

    The song

    Each block plays a section. Drag blocks to change the order, and drag a section in from the row below. Click a block to set who plays in it; double-click to compose it.

    Sections

    The pieces the song is made of. Open one in Compose to write its chords, melody and drums.

    Drag a track to the part it plays in this section, or click it and then click the row. Gold: not its usual part. The sliders: how loud it is in this section.

    Note length

    Click an empty spot for a note; double-click a note to take it away. Click notes to select them (Shift for more), or drag a box around them. Drag them to move, with Option to copy; drag a note's right edge to make it longer. Right-click for octaves, copy and paste. Keys: Delete, arrows (Shift+↑↓: an octave), ⌘C ⌘V, ⌘D duplicate, ⌘A all. Gold rows are the notes of the chord playing there.

    Click a square for a hit on that half beat. Timpani play the root of the chord.

    The band

    Each track plays one part with one sound, in every section. In Arrange you choose who plays in each block.
