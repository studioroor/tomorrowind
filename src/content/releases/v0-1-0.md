The first public release of Tomorrowind: found and run a House Hlaalu colony anywhere in Morrowind.

This is an early version of the core game. There is no island or quest yet: you found the colony wherever you're standing. Expect rough edges, and balance that still needs work.

## What's in it

- **Settlers and jobs:** mining, farming, woodcutting, smelting, building and guarding, with settlers who haul materials and fetch what's missing by themselves.
- **Freeform building:** place buildings anywhere, with a choice of models and adjustable height. On slopes they get timber pilings and need extra work.
- **Construction you can watch:** materials pile up on site and buildings rise out of the ground.
- **Furnishings:** campfires, lamps, chairs, tables, bookshelves, chests, beds, walk-in tents, and walls and fences that snap together and curve.
- **A daily life:** everyone keeps their own hours for work, meals, free time and sleep.
- **Defense:** guards fight attackers, and with a stockade they take them prisoner for forced labour.
- **Progress while you're away:** the colony keeps working, with a summary when you return.
