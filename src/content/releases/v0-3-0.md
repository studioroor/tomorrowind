The colony now works the land around it. Woodcutters fell real trees, and farmers plant, tend and harvest their fields plot by plot.

## What's new

- **Woodcutters fell real trees:** a Woodcutter's Camp has to stand near a tree. Its woodcutters walk out to the trees around it, the game's own or ones you planted, and chop at the trunk. When a tree's timber runs out, it topples over away from the woodcutter and leaves a stump.
- **Trees grow back:** half a day later the stump sprouts, and the sapling grows back to full size in two days. A handful of trees keeps one woodcutter busy, so more woodcutters need more trees. The camp shows how many of its trees are ready, growing or felled.
- **Plant trees and groves:** the Furnish tab has a new **Nature** group with a single **Tree** and a **Grove** of five saplings, for wood or just for looks. They're free; a builder plants them. Pick the kind with **Left/Right**: Ascadian Isles, West Gash, Bitter Coast, Grazelands or Azura's Coast. The kind that grows around your colony comes first.
- **Crops that grow:** a farm's field is 24 plots. Farmers harvest a ripe plot and carry the food home, plant the bare plots, and tend whatever is growing, which makes it grow faster. Neglected crops only grow slower; nothing dies. The farm shows its field, like "crops: 6 ripe, 12 growing, 6 bare".
- **Kwama nests refill themselves:** nobody plants eggs. The kwama lay new ones, and the farmers gather and tend.
- **Pilings along the walls:** on sloping ground, the foundation pilings stand along a building's walls. They go in one by one during construction and are driven down until the floor hides them.
- **Sleep in your beds:** using a bed or bedroll you built opens the rest window.
- **Shelters:** a hide canopy and a small hide canopy to put furniture under, in the **Structures** group of the Furnish tab.

## Balance

- One farmer still makes about 60 Food a day. A full farm of three makes about 150 instead of 180: the field itself is the limit, so a third farmer adds a little less.
- A new farm starts with a mixed field, a few plots already ripe, so food comes in straight away.

## Updating from 0.2

Quit the game and replace your `tomorrowind` folder with the new one. Your saves keep working:

- When a save loads, the colony finds the trees around it.
- Existing farms get a field like a new farm's.
- A Woodcutter's Camp with no trees near it at all keeps working at its stump.
