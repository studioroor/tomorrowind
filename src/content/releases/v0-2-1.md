A small update to 0.2 with more freedom when placing things.

## What's new

- **Move furnishings:** look at a lamp, rug, chair, set of stairs, walkway or rope bridge and press **M** (or **Move** in the ledger). It goes straight to the new spot, free, with no scaffolding. Before, you had to delete it and place it again.
- **No second charge:** moving something you bought with gold, like a tapestry or candles, doesn't cost anything.
- **Stairs, walkways and bridges don't block buildings:** a house with a bridge resting on its roof or stairs against its wall can now be placed and moved.
- **Fine adjustments:** hold **Shift** while placing to turn and raise five times slower.

## Updating from 0.2.0

Quit the game and replace your `tomorrowind` folder with the new one. Your 0.2.0 saves keep working: this update changes no saved data.
