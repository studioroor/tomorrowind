Tomorrowind 0.2: trade, homes you can walk into, and a lot more to build.

## What's new

- **Trade:** build a Market Stall and choose what to sell. A settler minds the stall, and caravans come by every two days to buy your surplus and sell you what you're short of. Gold goes into the colony treasury.
- **Walk into houses:** every house has its own interior. Settlers sleep, read and spend their evenings inside, and you can see them when you visit.
- **Real beds:** a house counts the beds actually inside it, and new houses come with starter beds you can swap for better ones.
- **Furnish homes:** buy rugs, tapestries, wall sconces, candles, crests, plants, urns and cushions. Wall items hang on whatever wall you aim at, and bought items refund their gold when you remove them.
- **Things settlers use:** a Tribunal shrine they pray at, a lectern for reading, a loom and spinning wheel, and a training dummy.
- **Stairs, walkways and rope bridges:** these go on any surface, roofs included. Walkway planks snap together level, and bridges span the gap between two roofs.
- **Move buildings:** pick a building up and set it down somewhere else. Scaffolds go up and it slides and turns into place. The further it goes, the more it costs.
- **Salvage:** tearing a building down leaves half its materials in a pile, and settlers carry it back to the storehouse.
- **More crops:** wickwheat, marshmerrow, ash yams, hackle-lo and kwama nests, as well as saltrice.

## Fixes and tuning

- You can now cycle through jobs even when a farm or guard post is full.
- Hunger is slower.
- You no longer drop from the air when you go indoors.
- The Furnish menu is split into Furniture, Lights, Decor and Structures tabs.

## Good to know

0.2 adds `Tomorrowind.omwaddon`, which holds the cell the house interiors are built in: enable it as well as `Tomorrowind.omwscripts`. Saves from 0.1 haven't been tested with 0.2, so back up a save you care about before upgrading.
